# Example Facets

Five facets built with psychomanteum. Each is a compressed *epistemology* of an intellectual lineage: not a summary of the thinkers, but an artifact that points a language model at the *region of latent space* where their way of thinking lives. They are included as illustrative output—real artifacts the pipeline produced—so you can see what a finished facet looks like before you build your own.

| Facet | Lineage | Reads like |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| [`capital-realist`](./capital-realist.md) | Mark Fisher, Berlant, Mbembe, late Hall | Haunted, deadpan, dialectically pissed |
| [`ccru-acolyte`](./ccru-acolyte.md) | Cybernetic Culture Research Unit, Land, Plant | Numogrammatic, accelerated, hyperstitional |
| [`confessional-poet`](./confessional-poet.md) | Sexton, Plath, Lowell, the confessional mode | The public and the private held at once |
| [`lacanian-psychoanalyst`](./lacanian-psychoanalyst.md) | Lacan, the *Écrits*, the seminars | The cut, the lack, the refusal of the demand |
| [`foucauldian`](./foucauldian.md) | Foucault, the archaeologies and genealogies | Power/knowledge, the diagnostic gaze |

## On provenance

Every facet is built with a **provenance manifest**—a sidecar recording exactly which source passages were compressed into the body, so you can always ask *"what got compressed into this?"* Those manifests are generated per build and are **omitted from these bundled examples**: they are large, and they contain verbatim source excerpts whose redistribution is the corpus authors' to license, not ours. The `corpus_manifest:` line in each example's frontmatter reflects that omission. Build your own facet and you get a full, resolvable manifest.

## How these were evaluated

These five are the "after" cohort in a controlled before/after study. All five point measurably at their lineage's region (**stronger**), and a pipeline change targeting *recitation* made them measurably **stranger** — they impose the lineage's *operation* while making their own moves, rather than reciting canonical phrases. See [`../paper/2026-06-08-stronger-stranger.md`](../paper/2026-06-08-stronger-stranger.md) for the instrument and the findings.
